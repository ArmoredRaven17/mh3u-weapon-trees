MH3U Weapon Trees

How to use

1. Pick a class. The bottom bar's first dropdown switches weapon class; the second jumps straight to one tree instead of drawing the whole class at once.

2. Pick a view. 2.5D is a tilted map you pan and zoom across (left-drag to pan, right-drag to tilt). 2D flattens that same layout with no tilt. 3D is a free orbit around one tree at a time in real 3D space (left-drag to pan, right-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, click a node to re-center the orbit on it). The camera button names what a left-drag does right now, Pan or Orbit, and the right button always does the other. Click it, or middle-click the stage, to swap the two buttons over. The 2.5D bar carries the same control with Pan and Tilt, and middle-click swaps whichever pair the view you are in has.

3. Click any node for its full stats — attack, affinity, slots, element (in brackets when it needs Awaken), and whatever the class carries beyond that: charge levels (with Load Up), arc shot and coatings for Bow, reload, recoil, deviation and ammo for the Bowguns, phials for Switch Axe, notes and songs for Hunting Horn, shot level for Gunlance, sharpness (base and Sharpness +1) for every Blademaster weapon — plus what it costs to upgrade into it, and to forge it outright where the smithy allows that.

Snip — with a weapon selected, a small circle sits on each edge leading into and out of it. Click one to hide that branch and everything above it. The weapon below a cut gets a dashed ring; double-click it to restore that cut (or select it and press Restore), or use Restore all in the browsing panel.

Center On Node brings the weapon you have selected back to the middle; Center On Tree goes back out to the middle of the tree as a whole, which is where the view sat before you clicked anything. Neither changes how far in you are zoomed, and neither drops your selection. In 2.5D, the camera button reads Pan or Tilt for what a left-drag does right now, and the right button always does the other; Default tilt snaps back to the starting tilt.

Each upgrade in MH3U is a weapon of its own, so every node is one weapon. A run of upgrades is drawn as one straight lane: the first upgrade the game lists for a weapon carries its lane on, and any others branch off to the side. On the map and in 3D each lane is named once, after its first weapon, on the branch where it starts; click any weapon for its own name, or switch to 2D, which names every weapon.

About / Attributions

An unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for browsing every weapon upgrade tree in Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate. A port of the author's MHGU Weapon Trees, made with Claude (Claude Code).

Weapon stats, recipes & upgrade trees: embedded from mh3u-collection-tracker, which reads them from the game itself (a personally owned copy) — no third-party database.

Weapon, coating, note and monster icons: the game's own, via the same tracker; weapons in the game's own Rare 1–10 colours.

Titlebar, theme picker, font & textures: shared with the author's other Monster Hunter apps.
